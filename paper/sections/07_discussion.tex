\section{Discussion}

Interpret configurations as combinations of conditions rather than as isolated
marginal effects. Distinguish universal core conditions from region-specific
conditions, and discuss substitution and complementarity only when supported by
stable solution patterns.

The discussion should separate three claims: configurations that remain stable
across Europe, configurations whose fit is region-dependent, and configurations
that fail robustness checks. Net-effect model output may be used only as a
comparison point, not as set-theoretic evidence.
