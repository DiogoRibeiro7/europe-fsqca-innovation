\section{Discussion}

Interpret configurations as combinations of conditions rather than as isolated
marginal effects. Distinguish core from peripheral conditions only where the
intermediate solution supports the distinction, and discuss substitution and
complementarity only where the pairwise sufficiency tests and the solution
structure support them, not merely where two conditions co-occur.

The discussion should separate four claims: configurations that remain stable
across Europe, configurations whose fit is region-dependent, configurations
whose status depends on which population the pooled estimand describes, and
configurations that fail robustness checks.

Portability is directional. A recipe that travels from Southern to
Northern/Western Europe is a different claim from one that travels in the
opposite direction, and each direction must be reported with its availability,
its fit, and its bootstrap uncertainty in the target region.

State plainly which population each result describes. If the unweighted and
survey-weighted estimands disagree, that disagreement is a finding about whose
innovation the pooled solution represents and belongs in the discussion rather
than in a footnote.

Net-effect model output may be used only as a comparison point, not as
set-theoretic evidence.
