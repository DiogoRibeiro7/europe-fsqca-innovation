Solution stability is reported with two similarity measures against the main
specification: Jaccard similarity over signed literals and Jaccard similarity
over whole configuration terms. The two diverge when specifications draw on the
same literals but propose different recipes, and both are reported.

Any robustness result based only on synthetic data must be labelled as a
pipeline check and excluded from substantive interpretation.
