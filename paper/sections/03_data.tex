\section{Data and Measurement}

The empirical analysis will use harmonised World Bank Enterprise Survey
microdata for European Union firms. This section must report the exact release,
survey years, country samples, exclusions, missing-data treatment, source
question wording, and construction of each raw analytical construct.

\subsection{Data provenance}

The raw WBES files are not stored in the repository. The empirical run must cite
the source release recorded in \texttt{data/manifest.csv} and the
release-specific mapping recorded in \texttt{configs/wbes\_variable\_map.yml}.
