\begin{abstract}
This manuscript scaffold defines the argument, data requirements, methods,
result slots, robustness checks, and reproducibility record for a comparative
fsQCA study of firm innovation across Europe. It intentionally contains no
empirical claims before the World Bank Enterprise Survey variable mapping,
calibration anchors, and final robustness specifications are frozen.
\end{abstract}
