# Novelty Review

Search date: 2026-08-10.

## Review Scope

This note checks whether the project has a defensible contribution before the empirical WBES release, variable mapping, and calibration anchors are finalized. It is not a complete systematic literature review.

## Defensible Contribution

The project should frame novelty as a combination of design choices:

- firm-level WBES evidence for EU firms, once the exact release is verified;
- six capability conditions linked to digital, human-capital, finance, internationalisation, management, and external-knowledge mechanisms;
- common Europe-wide calibration anchors used before regional comparison;
- explicit portability tests of pan-European solution terms across macroregions and countries;
- symmetric reporting of outcome and negated-outcome analyses;
- reproducible diagnostics for calibration, truth-table diversity, contradictions, robustness, and Python-R validation.

## Claim Boundary

Do not claim that the paper is the only European innovation fsQCA study. The nearby literature already includes European regional innovation fsQCA work.

The stronger and currently defensible claim is narrower: the project tests whether firm-level capability configurations derived from harmonized WBES data are portable across European macroregions under a common calibration design.
